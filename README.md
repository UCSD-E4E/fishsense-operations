# Aqua3D/FishSense Operations Section
## Mission Statement
This section has primary responsibility for the assembly, test, deployment, and maintenance of operational Aqua3D/FishSense assets, including software systems, camera systems, and data storage systems.

## Structure
The Operations Section will be headed by a section lead who will have control over the training requirements of all members, ensure routine training events are conducted to maintain skills, ensure procedures are in place to build up core skills for incoming members, and takes final responsibility for delivery of assets to end users in the proper timeline.  This person should have a working knowledge of the entire Aqua3D/FishSense system, and have appropriate background/training in cyberinfrastructure operations, field operations, and development operations.

Members of the Operations Section will take on individual discipline roles, including but not limited to field ops and devops.

Devops personnel take on responsibility for ensuring that critical systems remain operational, that data assets are properly managed and catalogued, and that procedures for conducting all relevant operations are properly documented.  These personnel should be familiar with cybersecurity, infrastructure as code, monitoring, and data management.  The typical background of such members will be computer science.

Field ops personnel take on responsibility for ensuring that camera systems are assembled, tested, and deployed properly, and that procedures for conducting all relevant operations are properly documented.  These personnel should be familiar with calibration concepts and procedures, field environments, and data management.  Possible backgrounds of such members include computer science, marine instrumentation, and data science.

All members should expect to participate in at least one operational event (6 hours) a month and maintain appropriate authorizations/certifications.  Such operational events may include:
- Camera assembly/maintenance (training/production)
  - Data management
  - 3D printer operation
- Camera lens calibration in pool (training/production)
  - Field work
  - Data management
  - Data processing
- Laser calibration in pool (training/production)
  - Field work
  - Data management
  - Data processing
- Data acquisition in pool (training/production)
  - Field work
  - Data management
  - Data processing
- Camera lens verification in ocean (training/production)
  - Field work/scientific diving
  - Data management
  - Data processing
- Laser calibration/verification in ocean (training/production)
  - Field work/scientific diving
  - Data management
  - Data processing
- Data acquisition in ocean (training/production)
  - Field work/scientific diving
  - Data management
  - Data processing
- System reboot (training/response)
  - Devops
- System upgrade (training/routine)
  - Devops
- System usage (training/routine)
  - Devops

The Operations Section Lead shall be responsible for scheduling all non-response operational events.  Once high availability systems are stood up, the Operations Section Lead shall be responsible for maintaining a duty roster for responding personnel.  The Operations Section Lead may, at their discretion, designate Team Leads to run individual events.

All members of Aqua3D/FishSense must participate in the Operations Section.  Exceptions will be made on a case-by-case basis.
